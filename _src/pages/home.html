How a project works

  1. Get to know the business

    We talk about your customers, what they search for, and what the site has to do for you: bring in calls, bookings or quote requests.

  2. Design the homepage

    I design the homepage from your brand, photos and story. You review it before anything else is built, and it sets the look for every other page.

  3. Build every page

    Responsive pages, checked on phones and desktops, with fast-loading images and text that stays readable on every background.

  4. Set up search and launch

    Page titles, descriptions, headings and site structure are set up for search engines, then the site goes live on your domain.
